About

I’m James. I grew up in the UK, studied product and industrial design in Scotland, and eventually ended up designing digital products instead of physical ones.

I still like making things with my hands, though. These days, most of the making happens on a screen.

I moved to Berlin in 2017 for a job that was meant to last a year. I’m still here.

In 2025 I became a German citizen and a father.

Outside of work, I try to spend as little time looking at screens as possible. I climb, hike and take unnecessarily long bike trips, usually carrying too much stuff and trusting the weather forecast more than I should.
